# Exploratory web-scan analysis — Phoenix 200 n=16, Phoenix II n=16

Actual per-image Lab D65, OKLab and linear-sRGB-Y distributions are in exploratory_statistics.json. exploratory-distributions.png shows red/blue stock groups, normalized within images and balanced across authors (five red-stock authors, nine blue-stock authors). It is an exploratory collection comparison, not an intrinsic film-response measurement.

Embedded ICC is transformed to sRGB with LittleCMS; untagged images use a prominently recorded provisional sRGB assumption. Per-image presentation-border/watermark masks are explicit normalized rectangles. Original file hashes are checked unchanged during analysis. The two mirrored lower-resolution press samples, three small previews and the generation-unconfirmed forest image are excluded.

Source composition is unbalanced: 12 red-stock images from ArtByPino, eight blue-stock from Taiyou. Lighting, content, camera format, exposure, lab/inversion/post-processing and web compression remain confounded. Author balancing does not remove these factors. No causal film-generation claim, grain/halation recovery, model accuracy result or Phase 3 fitting follows from the displayed curves.

Source URLs, author credits, rights uncertainty and attribution remain in the manifest. Non-CC source images are private reference files, not repository assets or approved training data. Publication/reuse permissions require separate review before any image redistribution or formal fitting.
